Reading is the beginning. The Work has always been passed along in rooms — people sitting together, practicing together, asking the questions that only arise when someone else is there to hear them.

In-person gatherings are being planned now: evenings and workshops for coming together around the ideas and practices of The Modern Fourth Way — to learn, ask questions, compare notes from the practices, and explore the teachings more deeply than a book can take you alone.

The first events will be held in northern Illinois. From there, gatherings will expand to other regions based on where interest gathers — which is exactly what the form below measures.

Nothing is on the calendar yet. Register below, and when the first gathering is scheduled, you’ll be the first to know.
